01 / ABOUT ME

An engineer with
a purpose.

I believe a student's opportunities should not be limited by their school's access to technology.

My name is Mohsin Manan, and I am an engineer passionate about using technology to address educational challenges. I am working on a project focused on connecting underserved schools with technology and digital learning opportunities.

My goal is to help bridge the digital divide and contribute to an educational future in which more students can explore, learn and develop the skills needed for an increasingly digital world.
